Neon Sun Productions is an independent production house founded by Rageswari Mahanta — model, actress and entrepreneur.

We tell stories with honesty and transparency, following the full arc of human emotion rather than smoothing it out. Nothing is glossed over, and no shortcut is taken around the truth of a moment.

Our mission is to reach audiences across the world with storytelling that holds a mirror up to the society it comes from.
